What's actually verified

Registered, funded, real

Registered as a funded childminding provider with East Lothian Council

To deliver funded early learning and childcare hours, a childminder has to meet the council's own standard for approval — it isn't a self-description, it's a status East Lothian Council grants directly, after checks.

Ask Emma and David directly for their current Care Inspectorate report. Every registered childminder is inspected — this page doesn't state a rating because we haven't seen theirs, and a real one belongs here rather than a guess.
